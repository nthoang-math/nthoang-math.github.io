Hoang Thanh Nguyen

Nguyễn Thanh Hoàng · Lecturer-Researcher, FPT University, Da Nang

My research interests are in geometric group theory and low-dimensional topology: quasi-isometric classification and rigidity, commensurability classification, the geometric relationship between a group and its subgroups (subgroup distortion, quasiconvexity), and groups acting on finite products of quasi-trees.
